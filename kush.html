The delay is where the customer changes.

A customer joins at 09:00 and receives email one. They buy at 21:00. Email two is scheduled for 09:00 the next morning. A purchase check made only at signup is now twelve hours out of date.

For an acquisition-focused welcome series, check that the customer has not placed an order since entering before each later send. Keep consent and suppression checks separate. Buying something does not grant marketing consent, and a refund does not make a customer a first-time prospect again.

Take the test into the actual account.

  1. Confirm the signup trigger, consent status and source. Use the metric names actually present in the integration.
  2. Read the filter at the flow level and any message-specific filters. In Klaviyo, profile filters are evaluated at entry and before actions.
  3. Use test profiles for no purchase, purchase during delay, opt-out and suppression. Inspect the expected next step and the actual skip reason.
  4. Preview the exact message with missing preference data, images off and a narrow screen. Open each destination.
  5. Record the approved version, reviewer and date. Recheck anything edited after approval.

Smart Sending, consent, metric delays, time zones and overlapping flows can affect a live journey. This tool isolates purchase and eligibility timing so you can discuss the intended rules before building.
