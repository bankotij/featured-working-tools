Worked example / 02

21% is a useful number only when the denominator is clear.

Take 1,200 customers who placed their first order in the same acquisition period. Wait until each has had 90 days to return. If 252 place a second order inside that window, the cohort’s 90-day repeat purchase rate is 252 ÷ 1,200 = 21%.

Do not divide this month’s returning customers by this month’s new customers. Those two groups entered the business at different times. A recent cohort with only three weeks of history cannot be fairly compared with a cohort that has had three months.

What a four-point change means

A scenario, not a forecast or a client result. The arithmetic assumes one additional second order per additional returning customer, unchanged order value and margin, and no displacement of another purchase.

Choose the intervention after you inspect the journey.

For a replenishable product, check whether reminders arrive before the customer runs out, whether the correct variant is available, and whether the reorder destination preserves that choice. For an occasional purchase, repeat buying may need a different product or occasion. A shorter delay is not automatically a better message.

Review delivery and support first. A customer waiting for a missing parcel should not receive the same next-purchase message as someone who has used and liked the product.

Write the evaluation before launch.

Record the cohort definition, order exclusions, observation window and treatment. Where feasible, compare a randomly assigned eligible holdout with the intervention group. Otherwise, report the observed change and the competing explanations: discounts, stock, acquisition mix and seasonality.

The calculator translates an assumed change into money. It does not establish that a campaign caused the change. Platform-attributed revenue answers a different question.

A brief you can use

“For customers with a delivered first order of our replenishable product, test a product-specific reminder linked to the matching variant. Exclude recent repeat purchasers and unresolved service cases. Evaluate second purchase within 90 days, contribution after incentive cost, and unsubscribe/complaint rates.”
