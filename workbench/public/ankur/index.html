Bring a file you can trust

Use one completed order per row, in one currency, with the customer's complete order history. Required columns: order_id, customer_id, ordered_at, net_sales. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD. Remove cancelled orders and apply refunds to net sales before exporting. Replace personal identifiers with stable anonymous IDs.

The first order in your file defines acquisition. If earlier orders are missing, existing customers may be counted as new. Duplicate order IDs keep the first row and are reported. Orders after the selected date are excluded. The tool counts the second order only, including a same-day second order, within the chosen inclusive window.

A customer needs the full observation window to enter the denominator. A partially mature month uses only its eligible customers, which may still be a biased subset. Compare complete cohorts when making a business decision.

This is descriptive order analysis. It does not attribute a sale to email, claim campaign lift or estimate profit. Your CSV is read locally and never uploaded.
